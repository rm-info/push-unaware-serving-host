This server sends scheduled Web Push notifications for Progressive Web Apps without knowing their content: your app sends an opaque ID and a schedule, the server pushes that ID back at the right time, and your service worker shows the text it stored locally.

Requests are reviewed by the operator of this server. You will get a status token to check your request and retrieve your key once approved. No e-mail is sent.
