PUSH = PUSH Unaware Serving Host · a recursive acronym for a host that serves things it knows nothing about

Scheduled Web Push for your PWA, without telling the server anything

PUSH sends Web Push notifications at times your Progressive Web App chooses. Your app gives it an opaque ID and a schedule. At the right time PUSH pushes that ID back to the device, and your service worker shows the text it kept locally.

The server never sees reminder text, user names or accounts. It is open source and self-hosted.

Is it for you?

  • Yes: notifications at times known in advance and computed on the device: reminders, alarms, recurring nudges ("every day at 08:00", "every 2 days at 21:00").
  • No: notifications triggered by server-side events ("you have a new message").
  • Works with Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, including iPhone and iPad (16.4+) once the PWA is added to the Home Screen.

What the server stores

  • Per application: its name, a hash of its API key, allowed origins, quotas, a maintainer contact.
  • Per device: the push subscription (endpoint and encryption keys) and a hash of its token.
  • Per reminder: an opaque ID, a schedule, the next occurrence.

Nothing about end users: no text, no account, no IP address. Pushes are end-to-end encrypted and carry only {"id","t"}.

Apple, Google and Mozilla do not guarantee delivery or exact timing of push messages. If your reminders matter (medication…), tell your users.
